# Code of Conduct

These are guidelines, not a legal code.

## What we expect

- Argue about code, not people.
- Assume good faith until evidence says otherwise.
- Give specific, reproducible feedback.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic.
- Do not post spam, harassment, threats or private information.
- Do not break the law.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit, close or remove contributions that ignore these
guidelines. Repeated or serious problems can lead to a block. There is no
committee, appeals process or enforcement matrix.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.
Use the repository Security tab for sensitive reports.
